# Functional requirements v1
Sources: user request and selection specification. Progress persistence and regression detail also comes from the existing user requirements passed by root.

FR-01: Pocket permits unauthenticated standard, daily and practice play with four plot families and twenty confirmed placements.
FR-02: Pocket previews scores, permits cancel before confirmation, explains scoring and resident requests.
FR-03: Pocket saves and resumes locally; a completed run offers score breakdown and downloadable postcard; standard signed-in progress persists once, practice does not update account statistics.
FR-04: Pocket provides flat and dimensional boards, reduced motion, keyboard controls and four themes.
FR-05: Market supports room creation, room codes, 2 to 4 human players, tutorial and host start with capacity enforced.
FR-06: Market implements ten rounds with movement, exactly one business action, optional bounded trade and resolved income/commissions, with no elimination.
FR-07: Market purchases, upgrades, supplies, commissions, bank exchange and rest are valid only in their proper phase and ownership/resource conditions.
FR-08: Market trades specify both sides, require recipient acceptance, permit refusal and expire; simultaneous or repeated commands must not spend or award twice.
FR-09: Two independent Market clients see authoritative room updates, recover from refresh or disconnect, and do not lose or duplicate actions under concurrency.
FR-10: Market final scores reflect reputation, commissions, stall levels and capped cash; results and rematch work, and match history records once.
FR-11: Existing navigation, game selection/search, social/invite, progress and game flows retain behavior after shared changes.
